LAVENDER SKY v01

Original procedural Blender world for Kulaworld's dreamy ceramic direction.
lavender-sky-v01.blend: editable world nodes and equirectangular camera.
lavender-clouds-4k.png: 4096 x 2048, RGB, lossless sRGB panorama.
Runtime copy: packages/client/public/sky/lavender-clouds.jpg, 2048 x 1024, sRGB JPEG.

360 degrees horizontally, 180 vertically. Seamless spherical noise; no ground or fixed gameplay floor.
Three.js: EquirectangularReflectionMapping, SRGBColorSpace.
Unity: use the panorama with a panoramic skybox material (Unity import not tested).
No third-party imagery or textures.
