CERAMIC KIT v01

10 standalone GLB models plus editable Blender source.
GLBs: one centered asset per file, Y up, applied bevels, embedded flat-colour PBR materials, no textures.
Blocks: 1 x 1 x 1 units. Balls: radius 0.4. One unit equals one game cell.
Key: upright along Y in GLB. Exit rings: lie on the XZ plane, +Y surface normal; root sits at the surface.
Locked exit includes a bridge across its opening as a shape cue.
Ball pattern meshes can be recoloured through their material slots.
The Blender file includes a labelled presentation layout and the earlier ceramic study.
Layout positions and rotations are for presentation; exported GLBs are centered and unrotated.

These are visual assets only: no gameplay or collision meshes.
The Three.js renderer uses copies in packages/client/public/models/ceramic-kit-v01.
Validated: GLB structure, single asset scope, centered origins, triangle counts, block and ball dimensions.
